          Book

          A classic novel, one chapter a day: its pages turn evenly through your reading hours.

          The next chapter comes up each morning; very short chapters share a day with the next one. Each book keeps its place: switch to another and back, and it picks up where you were. Boards that start a book on the same day show the same page at the same time.

          Word of the day

          A new word every day, the same on every board. It changes at midnight.

          The board shows the word, what it means, and an example. French words also show the example in English.
