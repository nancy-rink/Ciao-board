Pair with your TV

  1. Open Ciao Board on the TV (is.gd/ciaoboard). Its start screen shows a QR code and a pairing code.
  2. Scan the QR code with this phone's camera, or type the code below.

Already past the start screen? Press OK on the TV remote, open What to show → Remote and choose Pair a phone.

The TV isn't answering. Check Ciao Board is open on the TV. If it still doesn't answer, pair again.
