Ciao Board widget

Put a still Ciao Board on your iPhone Home Screen: Small, Medium or Large. It uses Scriptable, a free app that runs small widgets. Tap the widget to open the full board.

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store and open it once.
  2. Copy the widget script.

    Open this page on your iPhone, then tap the button.

  3. Add it to Scriptable.

    Tap + at the top right, paste, and name the script Ciao Board at the top. Tap Done.

  4. Add the widget.

    Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, find Scriptable, pick the Large size, and tap Add Widget.

  5. Choose the script.

    Touch and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, set Script to Ciao Board, and type what to show in Parameter (see below). Leave When Interacting as it is.

What to type in Parameter

(leave it empty)BBC top stories, a new headline at each refresh
news worldA BBC section: top, world, uk, business, technology, science or sport
clockThe time and date
art heartA picture: heart, sunset, rainbow, smile, cake, ciao, italy or norway
artA different picture each day
CIAO NANCY / 7:45Anything else is a message. / starts a new line, and {r} {o} {y} {g} {b} {v} {w} are color tiles.
… @daylightEnd with @ and a style to change the look, for example clock @daylight or CIAO! @metro. All 26 board styles work.

iPhone decides when widgets refresh, usually every 15 to 30 minutes, and widgets can't animate. So the clock's minutes lag and nothing flips; tap the widget for the real board. To update the widget later, copy the script again and paste it over the old one in Scriptable.
